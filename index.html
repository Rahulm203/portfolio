01 — Mobile app · UI/UX · Figma

DripMate

Where AI meets couture
Try the prototype Open in Figma
The problem

Some people find it genuinely hard to put an outfit together. Endless options and no guidance turn getting dressed into a daily struggle.

The idea

DripMate curates complete looks, clothes and accessories together, for a specific occasion like a beach day or a date, so the user simply picks a look and unlocks the fit.

My role

Concept, UI design and interactive prototype in Figma, from the brand tone of the welcome screen to the editorial outfit layouts.

02 — Presentation design · Canva

The Psychology of Color in Movies

How grading shapes emotion
View the full deck
The story

A deck on how filmmakers use colour to control what we feel, breaking down films like Life of Pi, where blue tones and warm golden light move with the music.

The design

A film-strip frame runs through every slide, with stills from each movie behind editorial type, so the deck itself feels like watching cinema.

My role

Research, writing and full visual design in Canva.
